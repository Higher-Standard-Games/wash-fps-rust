# Privacy Notice

**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

**The short version:** we keep a name, a fingerprint of your key, your matches and the demos they make. Demos are public and keep you in them. Write to **washoi@higherstandardgames.com** about anything here.

## Who we are

Washiki Washoi and washoi.net are run by **Higher Standard Games** ("we"). We decide what is kept and why. For any question or request about your data, write to **washoi@higherstandardgames.com**. That address is private: you don't have to ask in public.

## What we keep, why, and for how long

- **Your player name and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of your key.** Never the key itself; no email, password or real name. *Why:* your account. *Basis:* our agreement with you (the Terms). *Kept:* until you delete the account.
- **Match results, stats and ratings.** *Why:* matchmaking, leaderboards and your profile. *Basis:* our agreement with you. *Kept:* while the game runs; leaderboards can be reset.
- **Demos of every match:** movement, aim, actions, your name, your account number and the match's chat. *Why:* so players can watch matches, and to train the game's bots. *Basis:* our legitimate interest, and other players', in an open record of matches and in better bots. *Kept:* permanently (see "Deleting your account").
- **Synced settings** (key bindings and game preferences) **and campaign progress.** *Why:* so they follow you between devices. *Basis:* our agreement with you. *Kept:* until you delete the account.
- **Fair-play counters:** for each match, totals per account: hitscan shots, hits and headshots, rejected and out-of-range commands, rewind attempts, movement checks, assisted input and how fast the view turned. Never your raw input; the demos hold that. *Why:* spotting cheating, and evidence if we act on it. *Basis:* our legitimate interest, and other players', in fair matches; used for that only. *Kept:* 90 days, or while a sanction they back lasts and 30 days after it, for an appeal.
- **Server logs:** connections, logins, names and account numbers. *Why:* running and protecting the servers. *Basis:* our legitimate interest in a working, secure service. *Kept:* 14 days.
- **Your IP address.** *Why:* limiting abuse (connections, logins, account creation). *Basis:* our legitimate interest in a secure service. *Kept:* only while you are connected. If we ban an address, we keep a keyed hash of it (not the address) until the ban ends, 90 days at most.
- **Backups** of the account database, for recovery after a failure: a daily copy kept 7 days and a monthly copy kept 12 months. A deleted account leaves them as those copies age out.
- **Bans and restrictions:** the account number, the reason, when it ends and who decided. A person decides every one; nothing bans you automatically. *Why:* keeping a cheater out, or out of public rooms. *Basis:* our legitimate interest, and other players', in fair matches. *Kept:* while it lasts and 30 days after, for an appeal.
- **Reports** you make about another player, or others make about you: who reported whom, in which match, and the reason given. The reported player is not told who reported them. *Why:* finding cheating and abuse. *Basis:* our legitimate interest in fair matches. *Kept:* 90 days, or with the sanction they led to.

Your key and your settings are also kept on your own device: in your browser's storage, or in a file the native game writes. The game has no ads, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies' servers.

## Who sees it

- **Everyone:** your name, results, ratings, and the demos with their chat. Anyone can watch a demo, and videos may be made from them.
- **Our hosting providers**, who run the servers for us. The servers are in the EU and the US: if you play on the US region, your data is processed there.
- Nobody else. We don't sell or share your data.

## AI training

We train the game's bots on demos and match events: movement, aim and actions. Chat is not used. If you object to your matches being used, write to us and we will leave them out of future training.

## Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account erases your name, the fingerprint of your key, your synced settings, your campaign progress and your fair-play counters and reports, and no key opens the account again. Only what backs a ban or restriction still in force is kept, until it ends. Leaderboards and results then show it as "[deleted account]".

**Demos keep you.** A demo is a record of a whole match, shared with every other player in it, so we don't edit or remove demos: your name and chat stay in the demos you played. If something in a demo harms you, write to us and we will look at it.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it (you can rename yourself in the game), to delete it (above), to restrict how we use it, or to object to how we use it. Write to washoi@higherstandardgames.com and tell us your player name. We may ask you to prove the account is yours, for example with your key, so never send the key unless we ask. We answer within a month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live, or to Finland's Data Protection Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi)).

## Age and changes

You must be at least 13 to play on our servers. When this notice changes, the game shows the new version before you play again.
